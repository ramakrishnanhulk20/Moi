Moi lets you give a friend their first real stock as a link. You pick a company and an amount, Moi buys the share and locks it in a vault on BNB Chain, and your friend opens the link, signs in with Google or email, and owns the share in about thirty seconds, with no seed phrase and no network fee to pay.

## Where the name comes from

Moi is the gift of money Tamil families give at weddings. Each guest gives, and the family writes the gift in a notebook so it can be returned one day. Moi does the same with stocks, and the notebook is BNB Chain.

## Who it is for

- Someone who wants to give a friend their first stock, and wants the gift to be a real share.
- The friend who has never owned crypto. They need no wallet app and no seed phrase. They sign in with Google or email, and Moi makes a wallet for them.

The shares are bStocks: tokens on BNB Smart Chain, issued by Binance's partner and backed one to one by the real security. The [FAQ](/docs/faq) says more about what a bStock is.

### How long a claim takes

About thirty seconds is the design target. It was measured once, by hand: on 9 October 2026 a phone claim with Google took about 25 seconds from opening the link to owning the share (gift 11, claim transaction [0x9342...c73d](https://bscscan.com/tx/0x9342094463ac3c5b725b061e6f6a8e9a40da25041e2f43931c28c37ef4b7c73d)). The judge claim worked the same day (gift 3, claim transaction [0x2964...0eb6](https://bscscan.com/tx/0x2964c2a78897a96976e4237bd103c6e7c163eabbf2a8a956e5e0873fba000eb6)). One run is not an average.

## What a judge can try in two minutes

The judges section on the home page hands out one real stock gift of about one US dollar to each judge. You press "Sign in to claim" and sign in with Google or email, type the judge code from our submission, tick the declaration that you are not a US person and not in a restricted place, and press "Claim my share". Moi sends the gift to the wallet it made for you and pays the network fee.

The steps, the limits and the errors you may meet are in [Getting started](/docs/getting-started#for-a-judge).
